# FireMUD Licensing Guide

This is plain-language guidance, not a replacement for [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) and not a reproduction of the PolyForm terms. LICENSE.md alone determines repository legal rights; a separate written agreement with Benjamin James Hook can grant additional rights to its identified parties. This guide does not narrow LICENSE.md.

## Repository Contributions

Code, tests, documentation, configuration, examples, or assets intentionally submitted by an external individual or legal entity require an accepted [Contributor Licence Agreement](CONTRIBUTOR_LICENSE_AGREEMENT.md) before merge. An individual contributor, or the human signing for a legal entity, must be at least 18 years old and have legal capacity to enter the agreement; an entity signer must also have authority to bind that entity. Contributions by minors are deferred pending New Zealand legal review. Contributors keep ownership of their work, while the CLA grants Benjamin James Hook broad rights to use it and to license it under commercial or proprietary terms, as well as under the repository licence in effect when it was submitted. Owner-authored work and the limited automation exception are described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md); applicable third-party licence terms remain in force. The CLA controls the inbound contribution grant; [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) continues to control the repository's outbound licence.

Original creator-authored game content merely created or hosted through FireMUD is not covered unless it is intentionally submitted to this repository for inclusion. Third-party or mixed material remains subject to its own terms; identify its provenance and applicable licence or documented permission for review in the pull request or through the licensing contact. Approval does not make a third-party portion a Contribution or expand rights. It may be included in Repository Material only under its preserved third-party terms or a separate direct grant from the relevant rights holder, with written approval from Benjamin James Hook or a Permitted Successor on file before inclusion. Contributor-authored portions of a mixed submission remain subject to the CLA when the contributor owns or controls the rights needed for its grant.

## Community Self-Hosted Lane

Subject to LICENSE.md, an ordinary community operator may inspect, modify, distribute, and operate FireMUD for noncommercial purposes, including a public or private hobby instance. This is the project's recognized and supported ordinary-community lane and a strict no-money product policy: a community operator may pay the operator's own expenses, but may not receive subscriptions, mandatory contributions, donations or tips, advertising revenue, sponsorship, affiliate income, reimbursement or shared contributions toward hosting costs, or any other financial or commercial benefit connected to operating the instance. Any descriptive FireMUD or FireDevOps reference remains conditional on the separate [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md) policy and does not imply official status.

LICENSE.md separately permits use by its named charitable organizations, educational institutions, public research organizations, public safety or health organizations, environmental protection organizations, and government institutions regardless of funding source or obligations resulting from funding. This guide does not narrow those rights. An organization should rely on that permission only when it falls within a category expressly named by LICENSE.md.

Ordinary community self-hosted instances have no FireMUD-managed commerce. An instance remains in the project's recognized and supported ordinary-community lane only while its operator does not monetize it or accept payment from others for operating it; [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) remains the sole authority for repository legal rights.

## Planned Official Hosted V1 Lane

An official paid multi-tenant FireMUD service is a planned V1 target under the FireDevOps project brand; it is not currently operating. If and when that service launches, Benjamin James Hook, as the identified planned hosted-service operator, would charge the tenant billing owner for hosting plans or platform subscriptions through the accepted hosting-billing flow. Official hosted creator content would be subject to the [Hosted Content Terms](HOSTED_CONTENT_TERMS.md) policy baseline only when the identified operator incorporates it into an accepted creator flow. Creator-to-player purchases, paid game subscriptions, tips or donations, platform fees on creator transactions, revenue sharing, payouts, and settlement remain deferred until a separate marketplace and settlement decision.

## Future Creator Marketplace

Marketplace support is not currently enabled; the future managed-commerce direction is not marketplace enablement. The tenant's Account-owned Creator Party defined by ADR 0180 would be the creator-side marketplace contracting party and the party to which creator balances are attributed; FireMUD does not create a second marketplace creator identity. That exact party must separately opt in for the identified operator and scope, and hosted-content acceptance, tenant roles, billing ownership, payer status, or payment do not enroll it or redirect earnings. Organization participation would require a current authorized signer under ADR 0180, and marketplace eligibility may be narrower than hosting eligibility. If the later launch gates are satisfied, only FireMUD-managed checkout may create FireMUD-managed entitlements or gameplay value, every FireMUD-managed paid creator-player transaction applies a disclosed platform fee before creator remittance, and a payout destination is separately verified and bound to the Creator Party rather than inferred from hosting billing. The fee rate and other marketplace launch requirements remain unresolved; unsupported party forms, organization commerce, and all marketplace implementation remain disabled. Unintegrated provider evidence cannot create FireMUD-managed entitlements or gameplay value. See [ADR 0179](design/architecture/decisions/adr-0179-firemud-managed-creator-commerce-boundary.md).

## Commercial Operator Lane

Unless LICENSE.md or applicable law expressly permits the use, an operator other than Benjamin James Hook that wants to offer paid hosting, advertising, business integration, monetized self-hosting, or another financial or commercial benefit connected to operating FireMUD must first obtain a separate written license with Benjamin James Hook. The separate agreement controls its scope and terms.

## Branding and Trademarks

The community, official hosted, and commercial code lanes describe software and service permissions only; they do not grant permission to use the FireMUD or FireDevOps names, logos, or official-service identity as branding. Community references and permitted fork or instance wording must follow [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md). Commercial code permission is separate from brand permission, so a commercial operator must obtain any required written trademark permission separately.

## Creator Content

Creators retain ownership of their original creator-authored game content, which is not automatically governed by the repository code license solely because it was created or hosted with FireMUD. The [Hosted Content Terms](HOSTED_CONTENT_TERMS.md) baseline describes the limited service-operation permissions that apply only after an identified operator incorporates the terms into an accepted hosted flow. Hosting does not transfer ownership or grant default marketplace rights; any creator opt-in to paid FireMUD transactions requires separate marketplace terms. Existing FireMUD or third-party material retains its own terms.

## Owner Development Support

Donations or sponsorship paid directly to Benjamin James Hook for developing FireMUD are a separate owner activity. They do not grant a community operator permission to monetize an instance.

For licensing inquiries, contact [licensing@firedevops.net](mailto:licensing@firedevops.net).
