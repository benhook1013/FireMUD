- Document's controlling [implementation-first brief](./task-briefs/document-implementation-first-corpus.md) and the [repository plan](/home/ben/src/FireMUD-unit-1b-admission-continuity/design/project-management/design-alignment/whole-corpus-authority-review-plan.md#implementation-and-review-method) supersede the former reviewed-and-routed section-exit rule. Accepted required pre-v1 work and prerequisites must be implemented before corpus completion; deliberate later work requires reason and activation boundary. Preserve earlier review evidence, distinguish corpus completion from PR merge and live activation, and return to provisional sections as dependencies clear.
- The final corpus closure attempt uses two independent unguided whole-section reviewers in parallel, including changed/unchanged authority, code, proof, and handoffs. Adjudicate the pair together. Material fixes require corrected-state whole-section review; isolated wording, fixture, or test fixes can close with focused proof and judgment. Repeated side noise can leave a section provisional, without detailed partial-coverage bookkeeping or tailored return prompts.
- Any numerical Hosted, CLI, or Luna allowance is a report-back boundary, not merge readiness, a stopping condition, or permission to leave useful accepted findings unresolved. Useful findings reset the applicable taper. Overseer must present the raw sequence and significance for human judgment before merging.
- A wake-capable sentinel owns a wait; the parent awaits its mailbox and does not poll or narrate unchanged timers.
- In merge-train work, prioritize scarce subagent slots for disjoint accepted fixes and focused proof. CI may run unattended while that work proceeds; check it at the stable candidate or when its result becomes the next blocking input. A passive CI watcher must not displace ready substantive work, and stopping that watcher does not cancel the GitHub run.
- Orchestrators delegate bounded, disjoint mechanical reading, inventory, implementation, and focused validation early instead of performing the bulk work in the main lane. They retain design decisions, finding adjudication, diff inspection, integration, and final evidence judgment; do not compensate for late delegation with overlapping agents. Before recommending a shared tooling or workflow change affecting multiple PRs or workers, inspect the existing mechanism, state the exact missing case, compare its smallest extension with a new mechanism, and obtain one bounded independent challenge of that comparison. The orchestrator still makes the decision; routine edits need no such review.
- Report Hosted raw found/accepted counts separately from current/outdated unresolved threads; zero unresolved threads is not raw 0/0.
- When local Docker is unavailable, use attributable hosted CI for the named cases when available and state exactly what executed; green checks without case-level evidence do not close the proof gap.

Review capacity follows the active front and is transferred only at a coherent handoff.
