# FireMUD Trademark and Brand Use Policy

This project policy describes permitted use of the FireMUD and FireDevOps names, official logos, and official-service visual identity. Benjamin James Hook currently uses the names as project and service branding; FireDevOps is a project brand, not a separate legal entity. This policy does not claim that either name is registered.

This policy is separate from copyright and software licensing. The repository licence, the Contributor Licence Agreement, hosted access, and any commercial code licence do not by themselves grant permission to use the FireMUD or FireDevOps branding. This policy is not legal advice and does not limit rights available under applicable law.

## Uses allowed without separate written permission

The following uses are allowed when they are truthful, do not imply official status, and comply with the conditions below:

- A truthful descriptive reference to the FireMUD project or software, including commentary, review, educational discussion, or a statement that software is compatible with FireMUD, when the reference is not used as branding.
- Preservation of required copyright, licence, and notice text when distributing the software. Preserving a notice does not imply endorsement.
- An ordinary noncommercial community fork or instance operating under the [Community Self-Hosted Lane](LICENSING.md#community-self-hosted-lane) may describe itself as `Based on FireMUD` or `Powered by FireMUD` when the operator's own name is the primary identity and a clear nearby disclaimer says that it is independent and unofficial and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Benjamin James Hook or the official FireMUD service.

For example:

> **ExampleMUD** is an independent, unofficial community project powered by FireMUD. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Benjamin James Hook or the official FireMUD service.

These permissions apply only while the stated conditions are followed. They do not permit use of FireMUD or FireDevOps as the primary identity of the operator's service, product, company, event, domain, social-media account, or application.

## Uses requiring written permission

Obtain written permission before:

- using FireMUD or FireDevOps as the primary or a confusingly prominent identity of a service, product, company, event, domain, social-media account, or application;
- using or adapting any official FireMUD or FireDevOps logo or official-service visual identity, or using confusingly similar branding;
- using FireMUD or FireDevOps in a way that suggests official status, sponsorship, affiliation, endorsement, or that a service is the official FireMUD service; or
- using FireMUD or FireDevOps in another confusing variant or commercial presentation not covered by the permitted uses above.

A commercial code or operator licence does not include trademark permission unless the written agreement expressly says it does. Commercial operators may not rely on the community permission above and must negotiate any separate brand permission in writing.

## Creator content and game identity

Creator-authored worlds, game content, and their names remain subject to their creators' rights and terms. A creator or operator may identify content as hosted on or compatible with FireMUD when truthful, but must not use that reference to imply FireMUD or Benjamin James Hook endorses the content or its operator.

For permissions or questions about use outside this policy, contact [licensing@firedevops.net](mailto:licensing@firedevops.net).
