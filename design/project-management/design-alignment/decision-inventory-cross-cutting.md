Status: Complete and independently coverage/fidelity-audited. This artifact is non-normative.

This ledger continues the completed read-only decision scan. It preserves the prior decision keys, sources, statuses, alternatives, ADR recommendations, flags, and review queue while allocating every decision to the exact capability identifiers in the [Product Capability Taxonomy](../../architecture/product-capability-taxonomy.md). The ledger contains 68 decision rows: 59 retained rows and 9 distinct evidence-backed additions. The counted source partition is exactly 22 paths. The existing [main consequential-decision-inventory](./consequential-decision-inventory.md) is intentionally unchanged; its nine ADR-ledger keys are cross-referenced below.

## Status And Review Notation

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `accepted-explicit` | Canonical design and/or an accepted ADR explicitly establishes the choice; this does not prove human consultation. |
| `accepted-implicit` | Canonical design consistently implies the choice, but adequate rationale or ADR evidence was not found. |
| `proposed/deferred` | Target design is stated but incomplete, gated, or deferred. |
| `conflicting` | Canonical sources imply incompatible current or target states. |
| `needs-human-review` | Product, security, operational, or architectural consequences require explicit discussion. |

`I/R` means importance/reversibility. `H/hard` is high importance and difficult to reverse. Source codes resolve to paths and exact visible headings in the source index. Secondary cells contain exact taxonomy IDs, not service ownership claims.
