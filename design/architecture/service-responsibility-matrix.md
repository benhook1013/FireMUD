# Microservices Responsibility Matrix

Checkmarks in this table indicate **participation** in a workflow. Rows prefixed with `Authoritative owner:` identify the sole invariant-enforcement or policy-of-record authority for that function. Cross-cutting contracts such as Multi-Tenancy are named explicitly in the function label; service checkmarks on those rows show runtime serving or participation and do not transfer the cross-cutting authority.

Platform Operations is a cross-cutting operational authority rather than a microservice column in this matrix: it owns object-storage, CDN, registry, and promotion-evidence infrastructure and delivery. Game Design remains the authority for publication coordination, release descriptors, asset lifecycle, CAS state, and purge eligibility; domain services retain participant data and digest authority. The first-party frontend is likewise not a microservice column: [FRONT-01](./decisions/adr-0144-stateless-first-party-frontend-application-boundary.md) assigns it an independently released static presentation artifact with no durable gameplay, session, authorization, or domain authority.

## Target Responsibility Model

The matrix below is the target participation and ownership contract. `Authoritative owner:` rows identify the sole invariant or policy authority; other participants coordinate or provide bounded evidence through documented contracts and do not create competing state authority.

## Implementation Status

- Live external operator availability includes the Gateway edge plus Logging & Admin domain ingress for reads, investigation, admission-pointer reads/audit, and prepared-upgrade proof reads. TCP Proxy does not participate in this HTTP operator read/proof-read path. `EvaluateModerationPolicy` is a separate live internal owner-boundary read consumed by Game Session and Social & Groups, not external operator availability. The target mutation rows below are normative contracts, not claims that every listed mutation is currently routable.
- The service-local `/moderation/actions` and `ApplyModerationAction` paths are present legacy transport stubs that hard fail closed after narrow tenant/admin checks, without dispatch or persistence; they are unavailable at external Gateway ingress, persist no separate fixed-category audit evidence, and perform no owner-side enforcement. The target path remains gated pending the action-family schema, shared cross-language `mutationDigest/v1` golden vectors, Account authorization-reference issuance, and Logging & Admin receiving-service validation/redemption. Synchronous `EvaluateModerationPolicy` consumption at `GAMEPLAY_ADMISSION` and `CHAT_SEND` remains a separate live owner-boundary read for Game Session and Social & Groups; those services own gameplay/chat enforcement, while Logging & Admin retains policy-input responsibility rather than consuming those enforcement decisions.
- Feature-flag overrides and scoped per-instance tick pause/resume are implemented-but-externally-gated ingress stubs pending their action-family schemas, shared cross-language `mutationDigest/v1` golden vectors, and Account-issued authorization-reference issuance/redemption flow and, where applicable, the target-only `privileged_control` issuance flow. That minimum gate is necessary but not sufficient for live success: supported owner execution also requires the complete durable, idempotent, fenced contract in [ADR 0048](./decisions/adr-0048-durable-idempotent-operator-write-execution.md). Version-upgrade preparation and admission-pointer CAS/cutover have internal implementation-participating, nonconformant preparation/read-then-write/prepared-cutover seams, but their public HTTP/operator mutation paths are target-only absent because no public route or Gateway write forwarding exists; the target atomic open/close CAS and prepared cutover remain unimplemented. Broader guarded tick enumeration, regional mutation, and reset/remediate orchestration remain target-only.
- Game Session `/sessions*` routes are current owner-local hooks; direct owner edge exposure is denied. The target external lifecycle ingress is `Gateway -> Logging & Admin -> Game Session` owner RPCs, but lifecycle forwarding is target-only and non-routable until its owner contract, minimum schema/authorization gate, and [ADR 0048](./decisions/adr-0048-durable-idempotent-operator-write-execution.md)'s complete durable, idempotent, fenced owner-execution contract exist. No complete current external `/sessions*` family is claimed.
- Versioned moderation propagation, hypothetical quota overrides, and broader tick remediation remain target-only/non-routable coverage without current executable owner routes. They cannot be exposed through Gateway or treated as Logging & Admin forwarding routes until the owning service contract and minimum schema/authorization gate exist, together with [ADR 0048](./decisions/adr-0048-durable-idempotent-operator-write-execution.md)'s complete durable, idempotent, fenced owner-execution contract for any family that forwards to an owner. The currently supported ingress remains limited to the live reads, investigation, admission-pointer reads/audit, prepared-upgrade proof reads, and internal owner-boundary moderation evaluation listed above; this target-only status does not remove or widen those current paths.
- The causal-read fence/composite room-view row is target-state only: current proto/request paths remain floor-free, and implementation/proof are unimplemented. See [ADR 0059](./decisions/adr-0059-causal-floor-cross-service-presentation-reads.md) and the [world-runtime-and-movement tracker](../project-management/implementation-tracking/world-runtime-and-movement.md) for current status. The accepted responsibility split remains Game Session allocation, Game Logic composition, and World Management/Entity Management participant provision.
- Plain-text gameplay remains the universal first-party WebSocket, generic text WebSocket, and Telnet/bridge representation. The versioned `PlayerOutput` projection is a Game Session-owned presentation contract consumed by first-party WebSocket clients; the frontend is only a presentation consumer. Classic-client semantic negotiation is deferred and unadvertised under [MCP-01](./decisions/adr-0145-plain-text-gameplay-and-deferred-classic-client-extensions.md); if later selected, Game Session owns semantics and TCP Proxy remains a generic bounded transport bridge.

The `<tenantId, gameInstanceId, regionId>` tuple in the tick and coordination health metrics row is the diagnostic scope Game Session must support through control-plane status, structured logs, and audit records. It is not a Prometheus label tuple: metric series must use bounded `scope`, `scope_bucket`, `region_class`, or equivalent operational buckets under the cardinality policy, while exact identities remain in diagnostic records.

For the edge-routable services in this matrix, participation does not imply that every mutation may be called directly by external tools. Per the overview’s canonical operator write ingress policy, external mutating operator workflows for moderation, quota overrides, runtime feature-flag overrides, admission control, session lifecycle, tick remediation, and version-upgrade preparation must enter through Logging & Admin. Direct external writes on other edge-routable services require an explicit bypass-safe designation in the owning service contract. Game Design tenant-scoped asset and template creator writes are the current architecture-level bypass-safe write class delegated to an owning service contract.

Service docs may not create new external bypass-safe write classes on their own. If a workflow is not explicitly allowlisted by the overview or this matrix, treat it as non-bypass-safe until the architecture docs are updated.

Route-review procedure:

1. Classify the proposed route using the [canonical route-review examples and traffic split](./system-architecture-overview.md#external-admin-traffic-split-canonical).
2. Confirm the matching participation and ingress rows below; participation alone does not authorize a direct external mutation.

- The exact current matrix rows are **External operator read/proof-read ingress** and **External operator write ingress**; their current availability and drift are recorded in **Implementation Status**, while the rows remain the canonical participation contract. Version-upgrade preparation is a mutation and belongs only to the gated write-ingress row; the read row covers proof reads, not preparation writes. Admission-pointer control has internal implementation-participating read-then-write and prepared-cutover surfaces, but the target atomic open/close CAS and prepared cutover remain unimplemented and externally unavailable; its ownership row must not be read as current external support or as an absent owner contract.
- Proposed route: `POST /api/session/game-sessions/{id}/feature-flags/{flagKey}:toggle`. Matrix check: `Game Session` participates in `Admin/creator API participation`, but the **External operator write ingress** row routes this workflow through `Logging & Admin`, so the direct external Game Session route is not allowed without a design update; current availability is governed by **Implementation Status**.
- Proposed route: `POST /api/design/templates`. Matrix check: `Game Design` participates in `Admin/creator API participation`, and `Bypass-safe Game Design creator writes for tenant-scoped assets and templates` delegates this domain-local creator write to the Game Design service contract, so the route may be edge-routable when Game Design documents tenant access, validation, and audit behavior.
- Proposed route: `GET /api/account/accounts/{id}`. Matrix check: `Account Service` participates in `Admin/creator API participation`, and the request is an external admin read rather than an operator mutation covered by the **External operator write ingress** row, so the route may be edge-routable when the owning service documents it as a bypass-safe read contract.

The moderation policy-intent row assigns target `POST /moderation/actions` policy/case/audit persistence to Logging & Admin; it does not assign runtime enforcement there. Active [ADR 0146](./decisions/adr-0146-owner-local-moderation-enforcement.md) (mapped from reviewed archive record `archive-ADR-0133`), together with [ADR 0141](./decisions/adr-0141-fixed-safety-restriction-categories-and-independent-lifecycles.md) and [ADR 0142](./decisions/adr-0142-bounded-moderation-appeal-cases.md), requires Account, Game Session, and Social & Groups to enforce through the ADR 0146 owner-local command/revision contract; each receiving owner commits its effective local projection. Routine enforcement reads those owner-local indexes and never calls Logging & Admin; the current `EvaluateModerationPolicy` seam is implementation compatibility drift and must not become a second authority. Currently, the service-local `/moderation/actions` and `ApplyModerationAction` transport stubs hard fail closed without dispatch or persistence; they do not persist the target fixed-category policy or audit records, perform owner-side enforcement, or produce target enforcement evidence, and they have no external Gateway route. Target owner-side enforcement, quota, runtime, admission-control/pointer-write, tick, and lifecycle mutations use the canonical Logging & Admin ingress and remain individually gated by their minimum schema/authorization gate and, where a family forwards to an owner, [ADR 0048](./decisions/adr-0048-durable-idempotent-operator-write-execution.md)'s complete durable, idempotent, fenced owner-execution contract.

## Notes on Redis Ownership and Participation

- **Independent version and admission CAS domains** – Game Design Service owns the version lifecycle state and `versionStateEpoch` CAS contract used for activation and rollback. Game Session and Logging & Admin may invoke that typed lifecycle API through their documented control-plane paths, but neither owns the version epoch. Game Session owns the admission-pointer read/preparation implementation and internal pointer-write/prepared-cutover surfaces for `{tenantId, worldSlug, realmSlug}`; the current writer is read-then-write/nonconformant, while the target atomic open/close `pointerVersion` CAS and prepared cutover remain unimplemented and externally unavailable. Gateway participates at the edge for operator/proof reads and write ingress, while Logging & Admin is the domain ingress for the gated target operation. Neither edge participation nor operator ingress transfers authority from Game Session. The two CAS tokens are independent and must not be substituted for one another.

- **Authoritative owner: Coordination Redis gameplay sessions (`session:game:*`)** – Game Session Service owns gameplay session bindings, lifecycle, and reset scope expectations for these keys. Other services participate only through documented shared helper libraries and key contracts; they do not introduce new gameplay session prefixes or modify TTLs/payload semantics without Game Session ownership and Redis design review.
- **Authoritative owner: Coordination Redis gameplay coordination keys (`tick:*`, generic `timer:*`, `retry:*`, `tick-executor-lease:*`)** – Game Session Service owns gameplay coordination schema and lifecycle for these prefixes; generic `timer:*` excludes Automation's `automation:timer:*` and `script-scheduler:*` families. Other services participate only through documented shared helper libraries and key contracts; they do not introduce new gameplay coordination prefixes or modify TTLs/payload semantics without Game Session ownership and Redis design review.
- **Authoritative owner: Coordination Redis auth sessions (`session:auth:account:*`, `session:auth:tenant:*`, `session:auth:token:*`, and `session:auth:generation:*`)** – Account Service owns the current legacy account/tenant projection families and remains the target owner of the issued-token and authority-generation projections, which are not current runtime guarantees. Their target lifecycle, revocation, and scope contracts are consumed by downstream services; Game Session does not mutate these keyspaces. No wildcard grant over future `session:auth:*` families is implied.
- **Authoritative owner: Coordination Redis connect-token replay and browser-revocation denial state** – Spring Cloud Gateway owns only this narrow edge replay-consumption and browser-revocation-denial state and its readiness/fence record under the canonical Redis contract. It does not own general gameplay sessions, Account auth state, or broader coordination policy. Reset/recovery consequences are owned by [Redis Reset & Recovery](./system-architecture-redis-reset-and-recovery.md); this row does not duplicate those mechanics.
- **Redis-backed automation ownership split** – Automation & Scripting Service owns its documented Coordination Redis scheduling/timer families (`automation:timer:*` and `script-scheduler:*`) and its Cache/Rate-Limit Redis queue projection (`automation:queue:*`), plus other Cache/Rate-Limit Redis buffers/counters. The former `automation:tick:*` staging path is retired and is not part of the live design or an active owned prefix; Automation hands gameplay work to Game Session, which owns the `tick:*` mutation boundary. Exact roles and reset/recovery consequences remain owned by the Redis ownership and catalog documents; these names mirror those canonical families and do not grant ownership of Game Session's generic `timer:*` family.
  Game Session and other services interact with automation via gRPC APIs, not by writing undocumented automation keys directly.
- **Cache/Rate-Limit Redis usage (caches, quotas, rate limiting)** – World Management Service, Game Session Service, Spring Cloud Gateway, Entity Management Service, Automation & Scripting Service, and Social & Groups Service use shared cache and rate-limit helpers backed by Redis. Logging & Admin has no direct Cache/Rate-Limit Redis runtime or target service role; it consumes Redis-derived health and metrics through service APIs/exporters and never receives Redis credentials. TCP Proxy has no current Cache/Rate-Limit Redis runtime dependency or shared-helper use; any future proxy cache/throttling participation is target/optional and non-authoritative. Game Session's current per-session limiter and presentation-cache writer are legacy disposable projections, not gameplay authority; the target limiter and presentation-cache roles remain defined by the canonical Redis cache documents. Prefix roles, owners, loss/reset tolerance, and invalidation semantics remain canonical in [Redis Cache & Rate Limiting](./system-architecture-redis-cache.md), the [Cache/Rate-Limit Key Catalog](./system-architecture-redis-cache-reference.md#cache-rate-limit-key-catalog), and [Redis Reset & Recovery](./system-architecture-redis-reset-and-recovery.md#reset-policy-matrix-prefix-summary); this matrix does not create cache authority. World Management is authoritative for invalidation semantics of world caches; Social's communication-history, fanout, and delivery buffers are rebuildable hints and never the authority for promised history, safety evidence, erasure, or retry receipts.

The Game Session, Account, and Automation ownership boundaries above are normative per [ADR 0009](./decisions/adr-0009-coordination-redis-ownership-boundary.md). The Gateway narrow edge replay/denial/readiness boundary listed above is defined by [ADR 0029](./decisions/adr-0029-single-use-gameplay-connect-token-carriage.md), [ADR 0171](./decisions/adr-0171-separated-redis-role-processes-and-owned-keyspaces.md), and [Gateway Architecture](./system-architecture-gateway.md).

## Notes on Movement and Moderation Contracts

- **Movement/location write contract orchestration** – Game Session orchestrates movement under tick/effect identity and owns per-session sequencing plus the current execution-region pointer; Game Logic computes deterministic movement outcomes; World Management commits authoritative room occupancy/location and validates its targeting token, while Entity Management applies entity-side consequences without owning occupancy indexes. The canonical stale-evidence and executor-fence mutation/barrier contract is [ADR 0054](./decisions/adr-0054-split-spatial-authority-with-causal-read-composition.md). For `ResolveLook` presentation reads, Game Session allocates the `CausalReadFence` from durable region commit authority and Game Logic propagates it unchanged to World and Entity.
- **Movement hot-path shape** – Under [ADR 0056](./decisions/adr-0056-one-hot-path-fan-out-owner.md), Game Session dispatches the fenced operation to Game Logic as the one fan-out owner, and Game Logic coordinates World Management plus Entity Management as its at-most-two authoritative participants. Neither participant may recursively create synchronous fan-out, and movement has no separate participant-count exception.
- **Canonical room-state causal floor** – The [Identifier Glossary causal-read fence contract](./system-architecture-identifier-glossary.md#cross-service-causal-read-fence-identity) and [ADR 0059](./decisions/adr-0059-causal-floor-cross-service-presentation-reads.md) own the requested floor and composite identity. Game Session allocates the fence from one Game Session-owned durable region-status snapshot after authority validation, using its operational `regionId` and `regionEpoch`, with `committedTickId` set to that snapshot's `lastCommittedTickId`; an accepted epoch starts at `-1` before tick `0`, and allocator state does not cross an epoch boundary. It passes the fence on `ResolveLook`; Game Logic propagates it unchanged to World and Entity, composes same-scope/epoch responses using served-through validation and opaque component versions, and rejects or retries responses behind the requested floor. Game Session owns request initiation, ordering, and transcript rendering/cache behavior, but it is not the downstream read orchestrator for `GetRoomSnapshot` plus `ListRoomEntities`. See the [RegionStatus timeline contract](./system-architecture-ticks.md#bootstrap-vs-stream-authoritative-timeline-source) and canonical room runtime contract in `design/architecture/system-architecture-overview.md`.
- **Item command runtime split** – Game Session owns text-session ingress and transcript rendering for player item commands; Game Logic owns the gameplay-facing item command RPC seam; Entity Management remains authoritative for item/container/equipment persistence, holder mutation, validation, and transfer audit writes.
- **Operator-action coverage split** – Admission-pointer `GET` reads, audit, and prepared-upgrade proof reads are the live external operator read ingress through Gateway and Logging & Admin. Version-upgrade preparation uses an internal implementation-participating path, not an available operator-facing mutation, and remains externally gated and unavailable pending its minimum schema/authorization gate plus the complete durable, idempotent, fenced owner-execution contract in [ADR 0048](./decisions/adr-0048-durable-idempotent-operator-write-execution.md). Runtime feature-flag overrides, admission-control/pointer writes, and scoped per-instance tick pause/resume remain unavailable pending the same minimum gate and complete owner-execution contract; `/moderation/actions` and `ApplyModerationAction` are present legacy transport stubs that hard fail closed without dispatch or persistence and remain unavailable for target fixed-category policy/case/audit persistence or owner-side enforcement pending their action schema, shared vectors, Account authorization-reference issuance, and Logging & Admin receiving-service validation/redemption. Game Session's `/sessions*` lifecycle routes are current owner-local hooks, not a complete external ingress family; target lifecycle forwarding enters through Gateway to Logging & Admin and then Game Session owner RPCs. Admission-pointer open/close CAS and prepared cutover have internal implementation-participating surfaces, but the external mutation contract remains target-state/gated and unavailable pending the same minimum gate and complete owner-execution contract. Target moderation enforcement uses owner-local indexed state under active [ADR 0146](./decisions/adr-0146-owner-local-moderation-enforcement.md), not a routine `EvaluateModerationPolicy` RPC; the current evaluation read remains implementation compatibility drift.
- **Tick remediation split** – Scoped per-instance pause/resume is implementation-participating but externally gated: Logging & Admin owns its ingress, target-state remediation API, automation policy, and audit trail, while Game Session owns the mutation in its gameplay coordination keyspaces. No live admin UI or automation is claimed until the minimum schema/authorization gate and the complete durable, idempotent, fenced owner-execution contract in [ADR 0048](./decisions/adr-0048-durable-idempotent-operator-write-execution.md) are implemented and proved. Broader guarded enumeration, regional mutation, and reset/remediate orchestration remain target-only until their owner APIs exist. Game Session owns mutation only within its gameplay coordination keyspaces; Account owns its auth projections. The Game Session mutation boundary explicitly excludes all Account auth families and Automation & Scripting-owned coordination families.
- **Replacement-instance compatibility preflight (External operator read/proof-read ingress)** – Game Session owns `ValidateInstanceCutoverCompatibility` orchestration and result semantics; Game Design, World, Entity, Automation, and Logging/Admin participate as dependency and policy providers for checks. Version-upgrade preparation remains part of the separate external operator write ingress.
- **Moderation policy propagation** – Logging & Admin solely owns `platform_access_ban`, `gameplay_ban`, `chat_mute`, and `chat_ban` policy definitions, policy intent, cases, bounded appeals/evidence references, policy-input/audit records, and the gated moderation mutation ingress; Social & Groups does not own those records or ingress. Active [ADR 0146](./decisions/adr-0146-owner-local-moderation-enforcement.md) (mapped from reviewed archive record `archive-ADR-0133`) requires owner-local enforcement with no routine remote checks. `POST /moderation/actions` and `ApplyModerationAction` are present legacy transport stubs that hard fail closed without dispatch or persistence; they do not persist the target fixed-category policy/audit records or perform owner-side enforcement, and they remain unavailable for the target path pending the action-family schema, shared cross-language `mutationDigest/v1` vectors, Account authorization-reference issuance, and Logging & Admin receiving-service validation/redemption. The separate live `EvaluateModerationPolicy` read supplies Game Session and Social & Groups at their owner boundaries and is compatibility drift rather than target authority; those services retain gameplay and chat enforcement respectively. Versioned policy snapshot/event propagation, pull-on-miss refresh, and broader enforcement remain missing; the applicable high-risk decisions fail closed when fresh policy evidence is unavailable. See the canonical moderation propagation contract in `design/architecture/system-architecture-overview.md`.
- **Ban taxonomy** – Account owns `account_security_lock` policy/recovery and `platform_access_ban` enforcement, including the required account authority-generation advances; Game Session owns `gameplay_ban`; Social & Groups owns `chat_mute` and `chat_ban`; Logging & Admin owns the policy intent, cases, appeals, protected evidence references, and audit rather than runtime state. Social & Groups enforces `chat_ban` at participation, message-send, and history-access boundaries while preserving essential system and moderation notices, as defined by its [API](./microservices/social-groups-service/api-contracts.md) and [runtime/data](./microservices/social-groups-service/runtime-and-data.md) contracts. The live `EvaluateModerationPolicy` read is consumed only at the Game Session and Social & Groups owner boundaries. The gated moderation enforcement mutation endpoint is unavailable and does not directly perform runtime enforcement. The fixed categories and independent lifecycles remain [ADR 0141](./decisions/adr-0141-fixed-safety-restriction-categories-and-independent-lifecycles.md); appeal outcomes are newer owner commands under [ADR 0142](./decisions/adr-0142-bounded-moderation-appeal-cases.md).
- **Communication owner split** – Active [ADR 0147](./decisions/adr-0147-explicit-communication-classes-and-owner-delivery.md) (mapped from reviewed archive record `archive-ADR-0134`) classifies world/gameplay communication for Game Logic semantic resolution and social-channel communication/mail for direct Social ingress. Social applies membership, moderation, audience, history, and delivery-state rules to an authorized plan; Game Session owns final connected gameplay transport delivery. No service claims end-to-end delivery for every class, and private platform communication is not exposed to tenant-authored gameplay scripts.
- **Social relationship, value, and history split** – Active [ADR 0148](./decisions/adr-0148-social-relationship-authority-and-entity-owned-value.md) (reviewed archive record `archive-ADR-0135`) makes Social & Groups the relationship/group/audience and envelope owner, while Account owns identity/profile policy, Game Session owns presence/transports, and Entity owns containers, items, currency, inventory, and attachments. Active [ADR 0149](./decisions/adr-0149-communication-type-specific-history-and-retention.md) (reviewed archive record `archive-ADR-0136`) makes history/retention/acknowledgement type-specific and Redis rebuildable only. The former room-local `SOCIAL-01` staging assumption is historical superseded provenance, not a competing contract; its communication/history/observer decisions are represented by active ADRs 0147, 0149, and 0150.
- **Observer and SHOUT boundary** – Active [ADR 0150](./decisions/adr-0150-closed-observer-views-and-profile-scoped-shout.md) (reviewed archive record `archive-ADR-0137`) limits gameplay observation to world communication, uses closed candidate-specific view classes, keeps ordinary gameplay `TELL` non-observable by default, and leaves `SHOUT` profile-defined and bounded. Area-versus-region policy remains deferred until a concrete profile requires it; no platform-global or invented default is implied.
- **Admin/creator API allowlist policy** – Gateway owns the edge-route allowlist policy; domain services own only the API contracts behind allowlisted routes.
- **External operator read/proof-read ingress** – Gateway is the external edge ingress and Logging & Admin is the domain ingress for admission-pointer reads/audit/prepared-upgrade proof reads; TCP Proxy does not participate in this HTTP path. Version-upgrade preparation uses an internal implementation-participating path, but it is not part of this read/proof-read ingress or a currently available operator-facing mutation: it remains externally gated and unavailable pending its minimum schema/authorization gate and the complete durable, idempotent, fenced owner-execution contract in [ADR 0048](./decisions/adr-0048-durable-idempotent-operator-write-execution.md) under the external operator write ingress. Game Session remains the authority for the underlying read/proof-read contract. Game Session's current `/sessions*` lifecycle routes remain owner-local rather than current external ingress.
- **External operator write ingress** – Gateway is the external edge ingress and Logging & Admin is the mandatory domain ingress for implementation-participating runtime feature flags, version-upgrade preparation, admission-control/pointer writes, and scoped per-instance tick pause/resume, but those mutations remain externally gated pending their minimum schema/authorization gate and the complete durable, idempotent, fenced owner-execution contract in [ADR 0048](./decisions/adr-0048-durable-idempotent-operator-write-execution.md). Lifecycle forwarding, versioned moderation propagation, hypothetical quota overrides, and broader tick remediation are target-only/non-routable participation contracts, not current routes; they may enter through Gateway to Logging & Admin only after the owning service contract, action-family schema, shared vectors, Account authorization-reference issuance/redemption flow, and any required receiving-boundary validation exist, together with the complete owner-execution contract where the family forwards to an owner. Admission-pointer open/close CAS and prepared cutover remain internal implementation-participating surfaces, but the external mutation contract is target-state/gated under the same prerequisites and is not currently callable. The live read/proof-read ingress and current owner-local/internal paths remain unchanged.
- **Edge admin/creator protocol** – External admin/creator APIs are HTTP(S) only at the Gateway edge unless a dedicated design update explicitly adds an edge gRPC contract. Internal service-to-service gRPC remains direct. External mutating operator workflows defined in the overview’s canonical operator action table must enter through Logging & Admin rather than directly through another edge-routable service.
- **Edge exposure default** – Unless a service is explicitly marked as participating in edge-routable domain APIs, its APIs are internal-only and reached through service-to-service contracts, not directly from external tools via Gateway.
- **Gameplay hot path policy** – Service APIs follow [ADR 0056](./decisions/adr-0056-one-hot-path-fan-out-owner.md): one ingress/dispatch step, one synchronous fan-out owner, and at most two authoritative participants across the transitive critical path. Every RPC stage, repeated call, retry, timeout, p95/p99 budget, fallback, and fail-closed outcome remains inside the measured contract; a third authoritative participant requires a new architecture decision.
