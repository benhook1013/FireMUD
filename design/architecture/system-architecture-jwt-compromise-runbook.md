# FireMUD JWT Compromise Runbook

This runbook defines the mandatory response flow for suspected compromise of JWT signing key material.
Post-restore hardening follows the event-classified trust-reset contract in [ADR 0155](./decisions/adr-0155-automated-event-classified-post-restore-trust-reset.md). The same hard-cutover key semantics apply when JWT/JWKS material was restored, the trust boundary changed, current external authority cannot be proved, or the event is a known or suspected compromise. A same-boundary PostgreSQL-only rewind may preserve current JWT trust only when live evidence proves that the current authority remained outside the restore artifact and is still correctly bound; otherwise fresh material is required. The universal restore quarantine, old-authority fencing, session invalidation, and controlled-reopen gates still apply to every rewind.

## Implementation Status

This runbook describes target-state behavior. The current runtime does not implement non-exportable signer delegation, Account-only asymmetric issuance and validation, issuer authority-generation advancement and validation, or the rotation/convergence evidence flow; existing HMAC/JWKS file behavior must not be treated as proof that this response flow is available.

## Post-Restore Disposition Boundary

Post-restore hardening records exactly one event-classified disposition for every applicable credential or certificate class: `rotated`, `reissued`, `rebound`, or `verified_not_restored`. The JWT-specific hard-cutover flow below is required for compromise scope, a fresh boundary, restored JWT/JWKS material, or any restore where current trust lineage cannot be proved. For a same-boundary PostgreSQL-only rewind, `verified_not_restored` is permitted only when live artifact-lineage and binding evidence proves that the current Account signing authority and validator trust material were not restored or rolled back and still match the target environment. That disposition does not preserve database-derived sessions, issuer generations, gameplay epochs, or other rewound authority; those universal reset controls remain mandatory. Missing, stale, contradictory, or ambiguous proof keeps the environment quarantined and requires fresh JWT material or same-operation reconciliation.

## Trigger Conditions

Run this flow when any of the following is true:

- Signing key material (`jwt-signing-keys`) is suspected exposed.
- Unexpected token validation patterns indicate possible key misuse.
- Incident response explicitly classifies the event as key compromise.
- JWT or JWKS material was restored or rolled back from the recovery artifact.
- The restore created a fresh or changed trust boundary.
- After restore, current JWT trust lineage or authority cannot be proved.

## Required Response Flow

1. Quarantine JWT trust surfaces.
   - Stop new Account JWT issuance and block JWT-protected admission/control-plane traffic.
   - Keep protected traffic closed until the replacement, invalidation, and convergence gates pass.
2. Run compromise-mode rotation.
   - Before candidate preregistration or reservation creation, Account atomically creates or recovers the durable `compromiseRotationOperationId` fence bound to `expectedSignerGeneration`; a uniqueness constraint permits only one nonterminal operation for that expected generation. Only the operation holding that fence may perform authenticated custody/controller preregistration. Preregistration generates the exact candidate generation, `candidateKid`, and `candidatePublicKeyFingerprint` and returns an operation-bound reservation identity/version; no placeholder, provisional key identity, or digest with missing candidate fields is permitted. The request carries no private material. Account validates and durably records the exact reservation on that fenced operation before computing the immutable request digest. The digest, subsequent custody/materialization CAS, promotion CAS, and final validation all bind and exact-compare the operation fence, reservation identity/version, and candidate key-identity tuple. An authenticated identity mismatch records the same operation's definitive pre-promotion rejection without promoting the signer generation, publishing or removing JWKS entries, installing a deny marker, or advancing issuer authority. Account then invokes the custody/controller's authenticated operation-bound cancellation for that exact reservation and records its terminal release evidence before completing the operation and releasing the uniqueness fence. An unavailable, ambiguous, or mismatched preregistration, cancellation, or later validation result remains quarantined for same-operation reconciliation; Account retains the fence, retries or reads back only that reservation under the same operation, and cannot start another candidate operation. Reservation expiry is evidence that material must no longer be used, not evidence of cancellation: Account must obtain authenticated terminal expiry/release readback bound to the reservation identity/version before completing the rejected operation or releasing its fence. A candidate that may have been materialized is instead reconciled and securely pruned through the same operation before release.
   - Have Account Service continue that one authenticated, operation-bound transition for the preregistered asymmetric signing generation. Account binds the existing durable `compromiseRotationOperationId` record to an immutable request digest covering the expected current signer generation, a `candidateGeneration` strictly greater than `expectedSignerGeneration`, the exact `compromisedKid`, `compromisedPublicKeyFingerprint`, `candidateKid`, `candidatePublicKeyFingerprint`, reservation identity/version, and the incident. The operation must reject the candidate before promotion or authoritative mutation unless `candidateKid != compromisedKid` and `candidatePublicKeyFingerprint != compromisedPublicKeyFingerprint`; a missing, malformed, or unavailable fingerprint is not usable evidence. The promotion CAS must match the current signer generation to `expectedSignerGeneration`, the operation's exact `candidateGeneration` to the candidate being promoted, the exact reservation identity/version, and both key-identity pairs to the operation record; only that CAS may commit the candidate. The final promotion/reconciliation validation must re-read and exact-compare the same reservation and candidate tuple before completion. The committed signer generation, candidate `kid`, and candidate public-key fingerprint must equal those exact operation values. A uniqueness constraint and compare-and-set fence permit only one nonterminal compromise operation for each expected signer generation, regardless of incident; a retry with the same operation ID and digest resumes that record, while a conflicting operation ID, digest, expected generation, reservation, `kid`, or key fingerprint is rejected without mutation. That one operation owns signing-generation promotion, Account-owned JWKS publication/removal, the issuer-generation advance, the hard-cutover deny marker, and the final convergence/completion decision. In target custody it uses the preregistered operation-bound candidate identity with the non-exportable signer, which returns only the operation-bound generation, `candidateKid`, exact `candidatePublicKeyFingerprint`, reservation identity/version, and non-secret cryptographic digest or attestation for the delegated operation. In the interim fallback Account uses the preregistered identity with the materialization controller to generate, CAS-write, or prune only the named private-Secret slots; that controller additionally returns the Kubernetes `resourceVersion`, CAS result, exact candidate public-key fingerprint, and private-slot pruning evidence. Account validates and reconciles the result applicable to the selected custody path, verifies that the candidate public JWK has the exact candidate fingerprint, promotes the generation, publishes the replacement JWKS, removes the compromised `kid` and compromised public-key fingerprint from authoritative JWKS without overlap, installs the operation-bound fail-closed deny marker for that `kid` and fingerprint, advances issuer authority, and owns the lifecycle decision. A custody, CAS, JWK, reservation, or fingerprint mismatch leaves the operation quarantined without mutation and follows the same reservation reconciliation or authenticated cancellation/pruning rule above. The deny marker is a local fail-closed rejection control, not a replacement for authoritative JWKS removal, issuer invalidation, or validator convergence. Account remains authoritative for validation, promotion, issuer authority, JWKS publication/removal, and public/private pruning policy. Rotation automation is observation-only and never receives private material. The private key must never enter rotation automation.
   - Account promotes the new signing generation, publishes the replacement JWKS with the exact candidate `kid` and `candidatePublicKeyFingerprint`, removes the compromised `kid` and `compromisedPublicKeyFingerprint` from authoritative JWKS, installs the deny marker, and performs the sole issuer-generation advance for this operation exactly once before validator convergence begins. Each stage is durably recorded against the same operation ID and both key-identity pairs and reconciled before completion. A lost response retried with the same operation ID and digest returns the already committed generation, exact key identities, JWKS publication/removal evidence, deny-marker evidence, issuer-generation evidence, and validator-convergence state without another promotion, cutover, or issuer-generation advance. A changed digest, expected generation, `kid`, or fingerprint conflicts without mutation. Account must reconcile the custody/CAS result, exact public-key correspondence, and Account-owned public-JWKS status before declaring compromise rotation complete. Do not overlap, retain, or roll back to a compromised key or fingerprint.
   - Before promotion, Account compares `candidateKid` against every retained active and retiring key-ring entry and binds the uniqueness read, retained key-ring version, exact preregistered candidate identity, and candidate reservation to the promotion CAS. A definitive authenticated collision or identity mismatch is a terminal pre-promotion rejection with no generation or JWKS mutation. An unavailable or ambiguous uniqueness, custody/controller, or CAS result is nonterminal quarantined state for this same operation; it is reconciled without releasing the uniqueness fence or starting another candidate operation.
   - The rotation Job/CronJob must never read or update `jwt-signing-keys` or write `jwt-jwks`; it only observes Account/controller status, runs validator-convergence checks, and records evidence.
   - Only a definitive authenticated invalid or mismatched custody, CAS, JWK, fingerprint, or candidate-reservation result before promotion is a terminal rejection/abort for this `compromiseRotationOperationId`. Account must durably record the terminal outcome (`REJECTED` or `ABORTED`), failure stage, expected and observed identity/fingerprint/CAS/reservation evidence, quarantine state, and proof that no compromised or candidate generation was promoted; issuance and protected admission remain denied. Account releases the per-expected-generation nonterminal uniqueness fence only after that durable terminal outcome and its no-promotion proof are committed. An unavailable or ambiguous custody/controller, uniqueness, CAS, JWK, fingerprint, or terminal-evidence response is nonterminal quarantined state, not rejection: the same operation remains fenced and non-replayable until authenticated reconciliation establishes a definitive result or durable proof. Reconciliation must not release the fence or allow another operation for that expected generation while the response is unavailable or ambiguous.
   - If promotion has committed but JWKS publication, validator convergence, or forward reconciliation is incomplete, the operation remains quarantined as `QUARANTINED_FORWARD_RECONCILIATION_INCOMPLETE`; it is not rewritten as a pre-promotion rejection and is never rolled back. Issuance and protected admission remain denied while the same operation reconciles forward.
3. Verify and record the environment-wide issuer authority committed in Step 2.
   - Re-read the same committed issuer generation and its authority-projection freshness/source evidence through one versioned Account snapshot. The response version or ETag binds the committed generation, source transaction/outbox version, observed projection generation, and projection status; recovery must not assemble those fields from independently changing reads. If the snapshot changes while evidence is captured, restart the read and keep the trust surface quarantined. This is the logical invalidation boundary: every validator rejects a registry snapshot whose issuer generation is older than that committed generation, regardless of account, tenant, or token profile. Physical deletion of old token records and cleanup of gameplay/control sessions are bounded best-effort follow-up work; neither is a correctness authority, and wildcard scans or deletion cannot substitute for validator rejection of stale-generation snapshots.
   The corresponding issuer-generation projection is an asynchronous outbox output, not an atomically committed part of the durable generation/event transaction, and is applied set-if-greater. Missing, stale, uncertain, or cross-version projection evidence fails closed. Account must record the versioned authority-projection freshness fence before recovery can advance. Reauthentication remains mandatory after the authority transition.
   - Treat compromise of the per-environment Account key as global for that issuer. Tenant-selective cleanup is not sufficient.
   - Do not treat the issuer authority-generation advance as a substitute for key rejection; an attacker holding the old private key can mint fresh claims.
4. Force validator convergence.
   - Keep protected traffic closed. Freeze a versioned validator inventory when quarantine begins, then refresh or restart every validator in that inventory. A validator added, replaced, or restarted after the freeze is quarantined by default and must register in a newer inventory version and prove the same convergence contract before protected traffic can reopen. Each validator must install or observe the operation-bound fail-closed deny marker for the exact compromised `kid` and `compromisedPublicKeyFingerprint`, and atomically evict or replace every cached copy of that compromised JWK before validation resumes; a cached copy must be rejected even if it remains locally fresh. Each validator must also prove it has observed the exact candidate `kid` and `candidatePublicKeyFingerprint` in the replacement JWKS and the current issuer authority-projection freshness fence. The final convergence check re-reads the inventory version; any membership or version change invalidates the prior completeness result until every current validator has supplied evidence. An unreachable registry, generation, or freshness-fence dependency remains retryable `AUTH_UNAVAILABLE` / HTTP 503 and must not be classified as revocation; once the authority is reachable, missing, malformed, ambiguous, stale, or mismatched evidence is authoritative invalid/revoked evidence and fails closed. A validator that cannot prove the required current state remains quarantined.
5. Verify convergence.
   - Confirm every validator rejects the compromised `kid` and `compromisedPublicKeyFingerprint`, and accepts the distinct replacement `candidateKid` whose public key has `candidatePublicKeyFingerprint`.
6. Stabilize, monitor, and reopen.
   - Watch auth failure/success metrics and 401/403 patterns for anomalous behavior.
   - Reopen protected traffic only after the complete evidence record is approved by an authorized responder, all applicable deployment preflight gates pass, and the durable owner/controller reaches its terminal success state: the Account compromise-rotation operation must be finalized for this flow, and any first-live or post-restore reopen additionally requires the recovery/deployment controller to report `phase=finalized`, `status=SUCCEEDED`, with its quarantine-release postconditions observed. Responder approval records the human decision but cannot substitute for or override a missing, stale, ambiguous, or failed controller or preflight result.

## Mandatory Evidence Checklist

Before reopening player-facing traffic, incident records must include:

- Incident/ticket identifier and responder/approver identity.
- Compromised and replacement key identities: `compromisedKid`, `compromisedPublicKeyFingerprint`, `candidateKid`, and `candidatePublicKeyFingerprint`, with proof that both candidate identity values are distinct from the compromised values.
- Durable compromise-rotation operation ID, preregistered candidate reservation identity/version, immutable request digest, expected signer generation, candidate generation strictly greater than expected, committed signer generation equal to that candidate, custody response, retained-key-ring `candidateKid` uniqueness read/version and promotion-CAS evidence bound to the reservation and both key-identity pairs, authoritative removal of the compromised `kid` and fingerprint, operation-bound deny-marker installation, cached-key eviction/rejection evidence, candidate JWKS publication and exact JWK/fingerprint correspondence evidence, final validator-convergence evidence, and proof that the issuer generation advanced exactly once; a same-operation retry must return this exact evidence and the single operation's completed outcome without another promotion or cutover, while a conflicting operation, digest, reservation, `kid`, or fingerprint must leave state unchanged.
- If promotion committed before convergence or reconciliation completed, the incident record must preserve the quarantined `QUARANTINED_FORWARD_RECONCILIATION_INCOMPLETE` state and forward-reconciliation evidence; it must not claim a pre-promotion rejection, rollback, or completed reopening.
- For a definitive authenticated custody, CAS, JWK, fingerprint, or candidate-reservation rejection/abort, the durable terminal outcome, failure stage, expected/observed evidence, quarantine state, no-promotion proof, and per-generation uniqueness-fence release-after-terminal-proof; an unavailable or ambiguous custody/controller or terminal write is recorded as nonterminal same-operation quarantine with the fence retained.
- Quarantine start and end timestamps plus the protected surfaces covered.
- Timestamped proof that Account Service authorized the operation and reconciled the authenticated preregistration and custody result for the selected custody path: for `TARGET_NON_EXPORTABLE_SIGNER`, record operation-bound reservation identity/version, generation, candidate `kid`, exact `candidatePublicKeyFingerprint`, signer custody/controller authorization, and non-secret cryptographic evidence; for the interim materialization controller, record those fields plus Kubernetes `resourceVersion`, CAS result, exact public-key fingerprint, private-slot authorization, and private-slot pruning evidence. The record must also prove that the custody result and published candidate JWK matched the operation's reservation/digest-bound key-identity pair, that Account validated and promoted the signing generation, removed the compromised public key and fingerprint, published JWKS, reconciled the required public/private pruning policy, and that rotation automation only observed the Account-owned `jwt-jwks` update through the control/status interface.
- Issuer authority-generation, authority-projection freshness-fence, and logical session-invalidation evidence from one versioned Account snapshot. The record must identify the snapshot version/ETag, committed Account source version/event, observed projection generation/status, and the exact scope that was fenced. Physical deletion of old token records and cleanup of gameplay/control sessions is bounded best-effort work and must be recorded separately; it is not required as cleanup completion before the authority-generation gate can be judged satisfied.
- Frozen and final validator inventory versions, last observed JWKS generation, observed authority-projection fence, and convergence proof that each current validator rejects the exact compromised `kid` and `compromisedPublicKeyFingerprint` and accepts the distinct candidate `kid` and `candidatePublicKeyFingerprint`. The evidence must account for validators added, replaced, or restarted after the initial freeze. An unavailable registry, generation authority, or freshness-fence authority remains blocked as retryable `AUTH_UNAVAILABLE` / HTTP 503; it is not evidence that tokens are revoked.
- Reopen decision timestamp and approver, plus the applicable preflight-gate results and durable owner/controller terminal state (`phase=finalized`, `status=SUCCEEDED`, with quarantine-release postconditions observed where a first-live or post-restore controller applies); responder approval is not a substitute for either machine gate.

## Environment Notes

- `production` and `staging`: mandatory evidence is required before reopening traffic.
- `hobby-self-hosted`: same technical flow applies; operators must still capture the evidence list in deployment notes.
