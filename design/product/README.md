# Product Documentation

This section is the canonical product documentation surface for FireMUD. Product documents define platform requirements and observable product behavior from player, creator, and operator perspectives.

Technical contracts and target-state service behavior remain in [`design/architecture`](../architecture/README.md). Architecture decision records explain consequential decisions and tradeoffs. Implementation trackers record implementation and proof status; they do not redefine product or technical authority.

## Canonical Sources

- [Product requirements](./requirements.md) – Canonical product requirements overview and scope.
- [Capability taxonomy](./capability-taxonomy.md) – Stable capability identifiers and product-oriented ownership categories.
- [User journey overview](./user-journeys/overview.md) – Navigation across player, creator, and operator behavior.
- [Player journeys](./user-journeys/players.md) – Observable player account, admission, gameplay, social, commerce, and data-rights flows.
- [Creator journeys](./user-journeys/creators.md) – Observable authoring, publishing, playtesting, customization, and live-update flows.
- [Operator journeys](./user-journeys/operators.md) – Observable moderation, recovery, deployment, observability, and platform-update flows.

Requirements and journeys describe intended product scope and outcomes. Follow their architecture links for authoritative API, data, security, runtime, and operational contracts.
